# Quant Masters: Reimagining a competitive learning platform for students

**Project:** Quant Masters platform redesign · **Role:** Senior UI/UX Designer [NEEDS INPUT — confirm role title]

> Quant Masters is a learning and placement-preparation platform that brings together practice, assessments, competitive exams, performance tracking and interview preparation.

---

## The Challenge

> The platform had the content. The experience needed a clearer journey.

Students moved between tests, results, notes, competitive exams and interview preparation, but each felt like a separate destination. The opportunity was to connect them into **one preparation journey**, where every screen leads naturally to the next step.

## The New Direction

> From learning portal to preparation companion.

The visual language was rebuilt around the Quant Masters brand: bold typography, rich blue gradients and dark navigation surfaces on the marketing side, and a calm, structured interface inside the portal where students spend their time.

The landing page sets up the promise, **Learn → Practice → Analyse → Crack it**, and the portal is designed to deliver it.

---

## Design System

> One blue for action. Colour only where it carries meaning.

A single electric blue marks everything a student can press or is currently doing. A blue-to-violet gradient is kept for moments that deserve attention, like a test in progress or a live challenge. Green, red and amber are reserved for status: answered, incorrect, needs focus. **DM Sans** sets the portal in a friendly, geometric voice that stays readable in dense tables and charts.

---

## The Student Experience

> One sidebar. Six areas. A clear path from practice to placement.

The portal's information architecture follows the order a student prepares in: practise, review performance, take on competitive exams, learn how companies interview, then study the material that fills the gaps. The sidebar groups 18 destinations into six labelled areas, so a growing product still reads as one place.

---

## Dashboard

> From a dashboard of metrics to a command centre for the next action.

Instead of a collection of numbers, the dashboard is designed around what the student should do next: **progress → performance → recommendation → action**. Every metric shows its change this week, every chart links somewhere deeper, and the primary action, *Take Mock Test*, sits above all of it.

---

## Test Discovery

> Pick up where you left off, or start something new, in one glance.

The practice library leads with the unfinished attempt: time left, questions attempted and a single **Resume Test** action. Below it, every test card carries its status first, so students can tell what's new, what's in progress and what's done before reading a title.

---

## The Assessment Experience

> Keep the question at the centre of attention, with a lightweight palette for orientation.

The test interface strips everything back to the question, its options and a progress bar. A persistent **question palette** sits to the side, showing each question's state at a glance, so students always know where they are without leaving the question they're on.

- **Answered** in green with a check
- **Incorrect** in red with a cross
- **Current** outlined in blue
- **Not visited** left neutral
- **Mark for review** always one tap away

---

## From Performance to Action

> Performance data shouldn't stop at a score. It should tell students what to do next.

Results and Analytics follow the same chain: **result → topic analysis → strength and weakness → recommendation → next practice**. Each insight card pairs a finding with a suggestion, and the chain ends in a single action, *Practice Now*, that turns the analysis into a practice set.

---

## Competitive Preparation

> Structured paths for exams, and a weekly reason to come back.

Competitive exams like **IBPS** and **AFCAT** each get a structured path with overall progress, a mastery streak and a run of mock tests. **Weekly Challenges** add a live, time-boxed contest, and the **Leaderboard** shows each student exactly how far they are from the next rank.

---

## Interview Preparation

> Beyond exams: from how companies hire to what to study for them.

This is where Quant Masters becomes more than a test platform. Students watch real **company interview** experiences, work through **interview prep** by round, and study from **session**, **technical** and **verbal notes**, each one resuming exactly where they left off.

---

## Structuring Technical Notes

> An information architecture problem as much as a UI one.

Technical notes span several engineering branches, each with its own subjects and topics. The structure works the same way at every level, **branch → subject → module → topic**:

- **Computer Science:** C, C++, ReactJS, DBMS, then topics within each
- **ECE:** Circuit knowledge, then analog circuits
- **EEE:** Electrical technology, then electrical machines

A branch selector sets the context, subject pills switch within it, and a numbered curriculum shows each module's topic count and study time, with every topic marked done, in progress or not started.

---

## Company Discovery

> As the list of companies grows, scanning stops working. Selection has to scale.

Quick chips still work for a handful of popular companies, as on Company Interviews. For company papers, where the list keeps growing, selection moves into structured filters for **company, role and difficulty** that combine search with selection. On every card, the action changes with the state: *Start Test*, *Resume* or *Retake Test*.

---

## The Complete Portal

> One platform. One connected preparation journey.

From the first mock test to the final interview round, every screen shares the same structure, components and visual language.
