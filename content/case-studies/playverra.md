# PLAYVERRA: Designing a play, discover & rewards ecosystem

> Playverra brings casual gaming, discovery, challenges and rewards together in one engaging mobile experience.

---

## The Challenge

> Turning a game catalogue into a destination.

A list of casual games is easy to build and easy to forget. Playverra had to give people reasons to **discover, play, return and earn**:

- Make discovering games enjoyable, not catalogue-driven
- Give players a recurring reason to come back
- Connect gameplay with rewards that feel meaningful
- Keep a growing catalogue easy to navigate
- Look like Playverra, not a generic game portal

---

## Evolution

> From a purple game portal to a dark, game-first platform.

The first version leaned on a purple gradient, gems as its currency and a lightning-bolt logo. The redesign moved to a dark-first foundation, one currency in Playverra Points, and a single lime action colour, so the games themselves carry the energy.

---

## Brand Identity & Logo Redesign

> A mark that feels like play, and scales from app icon to splash.

The logo was redesigned alongside the product. A **P** fused with a check and play gesture replaces the old lightning bolt, paired with a bold geometric **PLAYVERRA** wordmark.

- **Playful** reflects gaming and entertainment
- **Dynamic** communicates movement and progression
- **Distinctive** is recognisable even without the wordmark
- **Scalable** from app icon and navigation to splash and marketing

The identity isn't a standalone logo exercise. It's the foundation the rest of the system is built on: **brand → product → experience**.

---

## Design Principles

> Play should feel effortless. Rewards should feel earned.

Five principles shaped every screen, each one visible in the product itself.

---

## Reimagining Home

> Home is built around what a player most likely wants next.

Instead of a grid, Home is a sequence of reasons to play, in order of intent. It starts with who you are and what you've earned, then gets you straight back into a game.

Further down, **Watch & Play** introduces games as short visual content, **Seasonal Events** surface limited-time campaigns, and rows like **Quick 5-Minute Games**, **Hidden Gems** and *Because you played…* keep discovery going.

---

## Game Discovery

> Curated, not exhaustive.

Discover moves away from a conventional catalogue. Search and category pills narrow things fast, and every card carries just enough to decide: genre, whether it plays online or offline, how long a session takes, and a rating.

---

## Game Reels

> Discovery, treated as content.

Watch & Play turns each game into a full-screen reel: the artwork and trailer lead, the essentials sit underneath, and **Play Now** is the one unmistakable action.

---

## Challenges & Playverra Points

> One currency. One clear loop.

There's no XP, levels or progression maze. Everything a player does feeds one number, Playverra Points, and that number turns into real rewards. Streaks and the daily challenge give a simple reason to return tomorrow.

---

## Rewards

> Rewards should feel earned, and easy to claim.

Rewards are presented like tickets: a recognisable brand, what it's good for, its cost in points, and a single Claim action. Filters split vouchers from coupons, and the remaining stock adds a little urgency.

---

## Profile

> Useful gaming information, not a social feed.

The profile leads with the player's points and a direct route to redeem them, followed by what matters in a game: games played, current streak, favourites and saved games.

---

## Visual Language

> Dark by default. Bright when it matters.

The interface stays restrained so game artwork, lime interactions and reward moments provide the energy.

---

## Design System

> Muted is navigation. Lime is the current state.

An almost-black navy base, one lime-to-green action gradient for everything you can press, and warmer yellows and oranges reserved for value and rewards. **Outfit** sets the type: geometric and rounded, with strong weights for an energetic but readable interface.

---

## The Experience

> One platform. Multiple reasons to come back.

Home, Discover, Play, Rewards and Profile aren't separate screens. They're one loop that starts with a game and ends with a reward.
