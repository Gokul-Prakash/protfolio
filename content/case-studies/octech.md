# Octech: Making consumer engagement feel tangible

**Project:** Octech website · **Role:** Senior UI/UX Designer · **Live site:** [octech.in](https://octech.in/)

> A complete digital experience for a consumer engagement technology company, bringing its capabilities, products, industries and campaign work into one coherent story.

---

## Overview

> The goal wasn't a prettier brochure. It was to make a company that sells **systems** feel as tangible as the campaigns those systems run.

Octech builds consumer promotions, loyalty programmes, branded games and AI experiences for enterprise brands across FMCG, Alcobev, Pharma, Retail and Banking.

I designed the website from the ground up: its structure, visual language, components, page templates and interaction layer. I framed Octech as a **consumer engagement technology company**, where campaigns are the visible layer of a system that keeps running after launch.

---

## The Challenge

> Easy to experience, hard to explain. That gap is what the website had to close.

Octech's offer spans programmes, platforms, an intelligence layer, AI modules and compliance. The site had to make that breadth **clear and easy to follow** for an enterprise buyer, and carry the playful energy of the work without undercutting the rigour behind it.

---

## Information Architecture

> Navigation tells you where things are. The cross-links tell you how they connect.

The structure follows the questions a buyer asks, in order: *what can you run, what is it built on, does it fit my category, has it worked?*

- **The homepage is the map.** Each block is a doorway to a deeper page.
- **Every page points sideways.** "Related" cards link products, intelligence and trust pages.
- **Templates over one-offs.** Products and case studies each share one structure.

---

## Visual Direction

> Red for the brand, navy for weight, and colour from the work itself, so the campaigns are always the loudest thing on the page.

One typeface, Urbanist, sets the tone: friendly enough for a company that makes games, confident in large headlines. **Octech Red** is used sparingly, for the brand and the next action. Deep navy and neutrals carry the interface, and each product gets its own highlight colour inside the system.

---

## The Hero Experience

> Instead of *describing* engagement, the hero performs it. The words say what Octech makes, and the phone and the world around it show it being made.

Two words stay fixed, **We make**, while the ending types in and cycles: *brands playable · loyalty rewarding · campaigns engaging · data actionable · AI personalised.* Each maps to one part of the company.

A 3D phone shows a matching campaign, and the environment behind it changes with each state, so the whole company unfolds in one continuous sequence.

---

## Making Complex Products Understandable

> Name the problem in the customer's words. Then show the product doing the job, in a frame they recognise.

Every product page leads with a plain-language problem and a real interface, from PromoGenie and AnalyticsGenie to SmartClaim AI and ShelfVision AI. AI is shown through what it makes and decides (AI selfies, personalised rewards, plain-language insight), never through generic robot imagery.

---

## Campaigns & Industries

> A mechanic is easier to understand once you've watched someone play it.

Instead of listing mechanics, a horizontal scroll shows them as campaigns people have played. Industries reframe the same platform as the problem each category brings, and every case study follows one arc: *objective → journey → what was built*.

---

## Motion & Interaction

> Motion here is explanation, not decoration. Every moving thing is showing how something works.

The 3D hero, typed transitions, a custom cursor, hover-to-open industries, horizontal capability scrolling and a feathered navigation all make the technology feel alive rather than described.

---

## Responsive Experience

> On a phone, the site should feel closer to the campaigns themselves, because that's where people play them.

On mobile the phone becomes the hero's centrepiece, the menu opens as a single rounded card, product tabs swipe sideways and hover interactions turn into stacked, touch-friendly cards.

---

## Outcome

> The result is a site that behaves like the product it describes: interactive, structured and built to keep people engaged.

These are design outcomes, not measured business results:

- A clearer picture of Octech's ecosystem
- Stronger, consistent product storytelling
- One cohesive visual language across 50+ pages
- A more engaging, technology-led brand presence
